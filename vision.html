Our vision

HealthSense is here for you.

We wanted healthy habits to be easier, not harder. So we built a simple tool that helps with grocery lists, meal planning, workouts, and meal nutrient estimates.

Why we made it

Fitness and nutrition shouldn't feel confusing. HealthSense starts with the idea that good choices should be easy to make and perform

What it is

  • Grocery list creator for diet goals.
  • Meal planner based on physical goals.
  • Workout curation for today’s needs.
  • Food scanner mockup for nutrient estimates.

HealthSense was made by people who wanted to improve their overall fitness and health and found it unnecessarily difficult. It shouldn't be like that. From that moment forward, our team at HealthSense set out to make the process of self-improvement easier. And from there, HealthSense was born. We're here to serve you.
